🎉 Ponnonam 2026 — Programme

📅 25–26 Sep 2026  ·  Birla Alokya Clubhouse, MPH & Central Garden
4:00 PM+
☕ Tea Stall
Unwind with a cup of freshly brewed chaya paired with a selection of special Malabar snacks. A perfect evening treat before the big day!
4:00 PM+
🛍️ Kerala Items Stall
Browse and shop an authentic collection of Kerala treasures — handloom kasavu sarees & mundu sets, traditional Brass & Bell Metal Décor, Nilavilakku & Uruli, Aranmula Mirror, Heritage Gifts and Temple Collections. A wonderful opportunity to take a piece of Kerala home!
🌸 Friday evening stalls are open to all residents — no registration required. Come, browse, and soak in the Onam spirit the evening before the main celebration!
8:30–9:00 AM
📸 Residents Gathering & Pookalam Viewing
Residents to assemble before the procession. Photo session at the Pookalam.
9:00–9:45 AM
🥁 Chendamelam & Onam Procession
Traditional Chendamelam and Onam procession through the community.
9:45–9:55 AM
🎊 Welcome & Inauguration
Welcome Address, Lamp Lighting & Opening Ceremony.
9:55–11:05 AM
🎭 Cultural Programs
Traditional Dance forms of Kerala · Soulful Medley — Apoorva & Deepthi · skit - The Onam Origins — By Kids · Onam Twogether — Pair Ramp Walk
11:05–11:45 AM
🥋 Kalaripayattu
Traditional Kerala martial art showcasing agility, discipline, combat techniques, weapon skills and physical prowess.
11:45 AM–12:45 PM
🎯 Onam Games
Lemon & Spoon · Biscuit Bite · Musical Chairs · Blindfolded "Onam Viral Walk" · Spot Registrations
12:00 PM+
🍛 Onam Sadhya
Served in batches for a smooth dining experience. Your ticket is your Sadhya Coupon — show QR or Ticket ID at the Sadhya Hall gate.
2:30–3:15 PM
💪 Tug of War
BA Residents — Gents, Ladies, Teens & Kids
3:15 PM+
🏆 Prize Distribution & Felicitation
Winners of games and other competitions; felicitation of participants and contributors.
📌 Onam Sadhya commences from 12:00 PM onwards and will be served in batches while scheduled activities continue in parallel.
